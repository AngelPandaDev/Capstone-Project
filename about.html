About InstaGrab

Connecting hungry people with delicious food, fast.

Our Story

InstaGrab began in 2023 with one goal: to make food delivery simple, fast, and affordable. We teamed up with restaurants across the country to bring great meals straight to your door.

Our team is made up of people who love food and technology. Every day, we're working to improve your experience and support our restaurant partners.

Today, InstaGrab serves millions of orders each month, delivering happiness with every meal.
