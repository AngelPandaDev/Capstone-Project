Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about InstaGrab

General Questions

What is InstaGrab? +

InstaGrab is a food delivery platform that connects you with thousands of restaurants. Browse menus, place orders, and get food delivered in minutes.

How do I create an account? +

Click the “Sign Up” button, enter your details, and you're ready to order. You can also sign in with Google or Apple.

What payment methods do you accept? +

We accept all major cards, UPI, digital wallets like Apple Pay and Google Pay, and select bank transfers.

Delivery Questions

How long does delivery take? +

Delivery usually takes 30–45 minutes depending on distance and restaurant preparation time. You can track your order live.

What are your delivery fees? +

Delivery fees vary by location and distance. Many restaurants offer free delivery for higher-value orders.

Do you deliver on holidays? +

Yes, we deliver all week. Some restaurants may have different hours on holidays, so check before ordering.

Orders & Refunds

Can I cancel my order? +

You can cancel within 2 minutes. After that, preparation begins. Contact support for special cases.

What if my order is late? +

If your order arrives significantly late, you may receive a credit. Reach out to our support team for help.

What if something is wrong with my order? +

We’re here 24/7. Contact support through the app or website and we’ll resolve it with a refund or replacement.

Account & Safety

Is my personal information secure? +

Your data is protected using strong encryption and modern security practices. Payment details are never stored on our servers.

How do I reset my password? +

Click “Forgot Password” on the login page and follow the instructions sent to your email.

Can I delete my account? +

Yes. You can delete your account in settings. All your personal data will be removed permanently.

Still have questions?

Contact our support team anytime
